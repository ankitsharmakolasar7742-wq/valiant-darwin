How the Biometric System Works

End-to-end security designed to protect democratic elections with artificial intelligence.

1

Registration & Photo

Voters sign up with their credentials and upload a reference photo. SFace extracts a 128-dimension biometric facial template.

2

Email Verification

Voter enters a 6-digit one-time password (OTP) to authenticate their email address before accessing the voting booth.

3

Live Liveness Challenge

Clicking a party initiates the webcam. The system presents a random challenge (Blink, Turn Left/Right, or Smile) to prevent photo spoofing.

4

Face Match & Vote Cast

Once face matches registered profile and liveness passes, the vote is submitted and an official digital ballot receipt is generated.
